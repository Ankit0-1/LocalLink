# Updated Marketplace Flow

```text
Vendor registers
  ↓
Vendor creates store
  ↓
Vendor adds grocery products
  ↓
Customer chooses store and browses its products
  ↓
Customer places a single-store order
  ↓
Owning vendor accepts and prepares order
  ↓
Vendor marks ready for pickup
  ↓
Delivery partner accepts delivery job
  ↓
Picked up → delivered → completed
```
