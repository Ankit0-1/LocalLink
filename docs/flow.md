# End-to-End Flow

```text
Customer selects a store
  ↓
Customer adds only that store's products to cart
  ↓
Customer checks out; order is linked to that store
  ↓
Store's vendor receives the order
  ↓
Vendor accepts or rejects
  ↓
Accepted order is prepared and marked ready for pickup
  ↓
Delivery job becomes available
  ↓
Delivery partner accepts, picks up, and delivers
  ↓
Customer and admin receive live status updates
```

The system never broadcasts an order to unrelated vendors.
